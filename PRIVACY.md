# winPaint privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-09_

winPaint is a local image editor. It does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

- **No network access.** winPaint never connects to the internet and does not declare the internet capability.
- **No accounts, telemetry, analytics or advertising.**
- **Your images stay on your device.** Pictures are only read from and written to the files you choose.
- **Local settings.** Preferences such as the theme, colors, tool sizes, window position and the list of recently
  opened files are stored on your device in `%AppData%\winPaint\settings.json` (inside the app's private storage when
  installed from the Microsoft Store).
- **Crash logs.** If winPaint closes unexpectedly, a technical error log is written to
  `%LocalAppData%\winPaint\logs` on your device. It is never sent anywhere. You can choose to save a recovery copy of
  your image to your Pictures folder.
- **Clipboard and desktop background.** winPaint uses the Windows clipboard and sets the desktop background only when
  you ask it to.

Uninstalling winPaint removes its settings. Questions: contact the publisher through the Microsoft Store listing.
